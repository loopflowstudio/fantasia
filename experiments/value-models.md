
```bash

```

512 learner transitions per cell. Stage watchdogs are 30 seconds, each cell is
limited to 60 seconds, and the process to 900 seconds including arena/report.

are fixed. Both raw checkpoints enter evaluation. EMA exports follow existing
trainer admission; they are not independent seeds.
