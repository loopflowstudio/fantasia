# Area → question hierarchy

All definitions here are agent-authored drafts dated 2026-10-09. `@1` is the
initial local definition, not a claim that Etude has imported it. Existing Etude
IDs below keep their meaning; new umbrella questions do not reparent its locked
questions. The ranked view may reference a question across areas without giving
it two canonical parents. Experiments attach to the question IDs in this table.
Evidence on child questions rolls up to their parent and area.

| Area (stable ID) | Question ID | Parent question, if any | Question / scope | Evidence that would answer it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluation (`evaluation`) | `trustworthy-evaluation@1` | — | Which evaluation results can be trusted as evidence about a method? | Paired-deal/seed uncertainty, frozen opponents and artifact identities; errors and invalid games retained. |
| Evaluation | `greedy-validity@1` | `trustworthy-evaluation@1` | Does greedy ranking predict independent full-game strength in the current matchup? | Frozen direct matches, population residuals, competencies and adversarial checks; no cross-world rank transfer. |
| Evaluation | `world-integrity@1` | `trustworthy-evaluation@1` | Does the world and viewer/replay boundary support the claim being tested? | Targeted rules conformance, hidden-world invariance and exact replay on the selected content; replay alone cannot prove correct rules. |
| Optimization (`optimization`) | `continued-learning@1` | — | What limits continued learning in the current Allies/Lessons recipe? | Replicated continuation on shared time/sample axes, controlled optimizer/schedule state and a non-saturated evaluator. |
| Optimization | `late-selection@1` | `continued-learning@1` | Does the absolute advantage floor throttle late useful updates? | From-parent floor contrast, selected actor/critic coverage, parameter movement and complete-game effects. |
| Optimization | `schedule-exploration@1` | `continued-learning@1` | Do rate/regularizer schedules or lost action support explain stalled progress? | Separate LR/tau interventions; action-support and entropy diagnostics by decision type; retained Adam control. |
| Optimization | `attention-optimizer-batching-filtering@1` | — | What is known about attention, optimizer settings, batching and filtering? | Controlled contrasts with named recipes/budgets, not mechanism attribution from a recipe sweep. |
| Optimization | `filter-scope@1` | `attention-optimizer-batching-filtering@1` | Does actor-only filtering outperform filtering actor and critic? | Paired seeds at endpoint and common cost, shared-representation caveat retained. |
| Optimization | `pooling-floor-interaction@1` | `attention-optimizer-batching-filtering@1` | Does floor removal interact with masked mean versus value-token aggregation? | Factorial effect and uncertainty; optimizer exposures separate from transitions. |
| Optimization | `aggregation-at-small-capacity@1` | `attention-optimizer-batching-filtering@1` | Does aggregation improve the small fixed recipe against the fixed scripted opponent? | Paired seeds at common updates and cost without changing action coverage. |
| Architecture (`architecture`) | `model-fit@1` | — | What representation and capacity buy useful strength per cost? | Capacity, pooling and information contrasts kept distinct; independent seeds and full-game outcomes. |
| Architecture | `larger-models@1` | — | When do larger models improve strength per sample, active time or dollar? | A capacity ladder at common observed support; final versus monitoring evidence separated. |
| Representations (`representations`) | `recent-history-input@1` | — | Does identity-aware public recent-event input improve the retained width64 value-token policy at fixed transitions and overlapping cost? | Current-ABI on/off controls, held-out games and initialization; extra parameters/information made explicit. |
| Representations | `semantic-generalization@1` | — | Does structure help play on unseen cards/compositions? | Identity and shuffled-structure controls on real play, not only opcode or static labels. |
| Data (`data`) | `learning-experience@1` | — | What experience teaches the missing Lessons-side play? | Deck/seat-separated full games, behavioral diagnoses and controlled curriculum interventions. |
| Data | `mirror-curriculum@1` | `learning-experience@1` | Does adding same-deck matches improve cross-deck play? | Paired cross-only/mirror-inclusive seeds with equal deck marginals; all four evaluation cells retained. |
| Data | `lessons-combat@1` | `learning-experience@1` | Is Lessons weakness caused by attacks, blocks or delayed interaction rather than deck limits? | Retained-game action analysis and selected-world correct-line interventions; aggregate win rates are insufficient. |
| Data | `training-opponent@1` | — | Does a stronger training opponent teach better? | Cross-evaluation over frozen/current/archive opponents with behavioral profiles and independent seeds. |
| Objectives (`objectives`) | `dense-reward@1` | — | Can dense rewards help without installing an unwanted strategic bias? | Terminal-only control and behavioral probes; historical shaping results do not choose current settings. |
| Teacher targets (`objectives.teacher-targets`, child of `objectives`) | `search-as-teacher@1` | — | Can search supervise a raw policy more cheaply than outcomes, and does repeating the loop compound? | Matched end-to-end generation/training/inference cost, held-out play and complete targets. |
| Teacher targets | `expert-iteration-compounds@1` | `search-as-teacher@1` | Does one affordable round with the student inside the teacher yield a stronger raw student? | Successive paired generations under matched total cost; weaker labels cannot be wished away. |
| Teacher targets | `belief-utility@1` | `search-as-teacher@1` | Do viewer-safe learned beliefs or conditional search improve complete play? | Held-out calibration/query mass and latency, hidden-truth invariance, then matched-cost autonomous play. |
| Compute (`compute`) | `machine-fit@1` | — | Which experiments suit each machine, and where is execution CPU or GPU bound? | Recipe-specific complete-loop cost, phase timing, storage and memory; no hardware choice from synthetic steps alone. |
| Compute | `execution-reliability@1` | `machine-fit@1` | Can bounded jobs survive disconnection and preserve complete evidence? | Failed attempts, source-bound recovery, publication/readback, deadline and cleanup receipts. |

`model-fit` references representation questions; `continued-learning` references
filtering questions; `learning-experience` references opponent and teacher questions.
These are relevance links, not additional canonical parents. Proposed additions
must be reconciled with Etude's full definitions on import, not silently assigned
an existing ID with different wording.
