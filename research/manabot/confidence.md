# Confidence in the system

**2026-10-09 · Codex (agent) draft · awaiting Jack Heart's review.**
Confidence attaches to a specific claim, not a whole subsystem's reputation.
A row can contain an established software property and an unknown scientific
property. “Established” below means demonstrated in the named bounded evidence,
not universally correct, current-source recertified, or approved by Jack Heart.

- **Established:** direct measured or executable evidence for the stated scope.
- **Suggestive:** an observed effect with insufficient replication, controls or transfer.
- **Unknown:** no adequate test in this map; distinguish not tested, inconclusive,
  unavailable evidence, and obsolete-world evidence.

## The practical confidence picture

**Build on the existing instrument**, while preserving its known boundaries.
The evidence does not justify rebuilding everything, declaring a capacity winner,
or explaining Lessons losses from an entropy scalar. It does justify separating
three questions every time: did the job execute correctly, did its policy improve
on the stated evaluation, and does that evaluation measure the capability needed?
Only the first has broad bounded coverage; the latter two remain research work.
