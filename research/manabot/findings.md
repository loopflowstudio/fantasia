# Draft findings

**Author: Codex (agent), 2026-10-09. Review: pending Jack Heart.**
All statements and synthesis in this map are drafts. “Established” means the
specified observation has direct evidence, not that a reviewer accepted the
wording or that it generalizes. These findings cite the register's pinned sources;
reported-but-unpublished results do not enter findings. Intervals are percentage
points unless stated otherwise.

## Not findings

The baseline's reported held-out +12 [9, 15] points over update 1,240, the restart
collapse, capacity cancellation/retention, and the recurring .88/.41 split remain
in the [pending register](experiments.md#pending-evidence). They inform which
questions to ask; they cannot silently become evidence-backed conclusions here.
No finding says that the plateau is real, the floor causes it, large models are
worse, Lessons is at a ceiling, or greedy is useless.
