# Assumptions

2026-10-07: No automatic retry of failed scientific attempts is justified by the
brief. Reconnect may retry reads and reconcile the same submission; failure,
pause, cancellation or missing evidence stops the serial cohort. Replacement
training needs a newly frozen attempt/protocol and remaining total allocation.

Use an OS-managed service on an explicitly chosen existing host, with permanent
host binding and no automatic cross-host failover. Do not rent a new controller
or move account/issuer secrets to a GPU worker. Live installation/recovery needs
a selected always-on host and renewable credentials; software/fault injection can
proceed locally without disturbing Mini.
