# Material assumptions

- The completed history study's `development` phase is retained verbatim; it is
  not relabeled a held-out endpoint cohort. Its two checkpoints do not establish
  a learning curve or improvement over an absent initialization evaluation.
- Final `lf context` reports memory, scratch and the Work seed within their
  limits; the earlier transient seed overage is resolved without raising limits
  or discarding Task direction.
