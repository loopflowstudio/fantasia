Actor-only has not demonstrated a benefit that justifies its extra cost. At the same 1,240 updates / 317,440 collected transitions, actor-only scored 45.33% [40.00, 49.67], versus 42.00% [35.67, 47.67] for filtering both actor and critic. Paired seed effects were +7, +7, −4 points, averaging +3.33 [−4.33, +11.33] points. The point estimate is below the older program’s proposed +5-point shortlist threshold and the interval crosses zero. That threshold was not separately preregistered for this mini cohort; it is not a confirmatory decision rule. The screen neither establishes equivalence nor rules out benefit at another budget.
