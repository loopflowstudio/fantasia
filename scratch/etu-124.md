TrainingRegime owns updates and learning schedules. LaunchSpec owns Machine,
lifetime_hours, spending_limit, artifact AccessScope and reserves. Experiment
binds explicit case/seed launches to monitoring. One admitted absolute deadline
bounds worker artifact permissions and the guardian. Collection stops at pause_at;
checkpoint, upload and cleanup reserves remain inside that deadline.

Bounded RunPod submissions are supported. Long allocations construct/compile but
fail admission without renewal and portable complete-state CUDA recovery. There
is no independent credential duration. An explicit STS policy deny bounds access
even when STS token validity exceeds the allocation. Cancellation stops compute;
it cannot immediately revoke an issued session. Extensions fail without changing
the existing allocation. Jack Heart clarified that these future capabilities must
not block supported bounded launches.

The executor saves raw/EMA/Adam and reports paused when the update target remains.
CPU recovery-enabled recipes retain exact update snapshots and explicit same-host
continuation. CUDA artifacts do not restore collector/RNG state; no automatic
replacement or restart is implied. Timeout inside a collection retains the last
complete recovery boundary and charges discarded collection time.
